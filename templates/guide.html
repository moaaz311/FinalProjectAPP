How to Sign In

A quick step-by-step guide to accessing your workspace

1

Access the Portal

Open the Power BI dashboard or report portal from your web browser. You will be prompted to sign in with your Microsoft account.

2

Verify Using Password

If prompted to verify your email with a code, click on 'Use your password' below the 'Send code' button.

3

Enter Password

Provide your account password and click "Sign In". Make sure your credentials are correct to avoid lockouts.

4

Stay Signed In

You can choose to stay signed in to reduce the number of times you are asked to log in. Click "Yes" to proceed to the dashboards.
